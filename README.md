SUPPLY AND DEMAND — AGORA (PLUTUS’): raise the price and sellers make more but buyers want less; the market’s one trick is to find the single price where those two wants exactly meet, the equilibrium — and push price away from it and a gap opens: a glut, or a shortage. ⚡ LIVE full-house instrument: the rising supply line and falling demand line crossing at p*, a sliding price opening a SHORTAGE below it or a GLUT above; the two lines in 3D; tesseract 4D + shadow + toddler. ◆ LIT: S(p)=a+b·p, D(p)=c−d·p cross at p*=(c−a)/(b+d); a fail-loud self-check throws unless S(p*)=D(p*) exactly and a below-equilibrium price opens a positive shortage. ▲ AMBER: straight-line curves and a single market are the textbook idealisation (real curves bend and shift); the equilibrium and the ceiling-shortage are exact.
